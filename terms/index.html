1. What Brainlet is

Brainlet is a note and memory app. You write things down, and later you can ask for them back. Your notes are stored on your phone. How your information is handled is described in the privacy policy, which is part of these terms.

2. Free use and the daily AI limit

  • Brainlet is free to use. Capturing notes, searching, the library, tasks, the widget and backups have no usage limit.
  • Some actions can use a language model: the optional on-device model, or an AI provider you connect with your own key. Without Brainlet Pro, these AI-assisted actions are limited to 20 per day, counted from midnight on your phone. Actions that don't use AI never count.
  • When the limit is reached, Brainlet still works: it answers and reads notes with its built-in rules instead of AI. The app shows whether a result used AI or rules.
  • We may change the free limit in a future version. A change will be described in the app's release notes and will never apply to Brainlet Pro.

3. Brainlet Pro

  • Pro is tied to the Google account you bought it with on Google Play. It returns when you reinstall Brainlet or install it on another Android device signed into that account.
  • “Forever” means for as long as Brainlet is offered on Google Play and the version you use supports it. If Brainlet is ever discontinued, the version already on your phone keeps working with Pro, but no new versions are promised.
